> 日本旅遊 3D 沉浸式導航：規劃行程、真實 GPS 帶路、天氣匯率地鐵一次搞定。

**完整說明 / Full description**（≤4000 字）
> Jangogo 是為赴日自由行打造的 3D 旅遊導航 App。輸入天數與地區，立刻幫你排好每日行程，並用沉浸式 3D 地圖帶你走到每個景點。
>
> ■ 自動排行程
> 選東京、大阪、京都、北海道，設定天數與旅遊偏好（文化、自然、美食、購物、親子、夜景），一鍵生成每日路線；短天數玩好玩滿、長天數放慢深度玩，機動日還會給你順路的在地建議。
>
> ■ 3D 沉浸式導航
> MapLibre 3D 底圖與立體建築，Three.js 打造的電動車／背包旅人角色，藍色發光路徑帶與 2D→3D 俯衝運鏡。
>
> ■ 跟著你的真實步伐
> 導航依你的真實 GPS 位置與步行速度前進（Snap-to-Road 吸附路線、平滑不跳動），不會還沒走到就被帶到終點；支援中／日離線語音播報。
>
> ■ 旅途實用資訊
> 即時天氣預報、台幣↔日圓即時匯率與換算、各地地鐵與交通票券建議、三種預算的住宿推薦。

## English

**Short description** (≤80 chars)
> Japan travel 3D navigation: auto itineraries, real-GPS guidance, weather & FX.

**Full description** (≤4000 chars)
> Jangogo is a 3D travel navigation app for independent trips to Japan. Enter your days and area, get a day-by-day itinerary instantly, and let an immersive 3D map walk you to every spot.
>
> ■ Automatic itineraries
> Pick Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto or Hokkaido, set days and travel style (culture, nature, food, shopping, family, night views), and generate daily routes. Short trips are packed; longer trips slow down for depth, with handy local ideas on flex days.
>
> ■ Immersive 3D navigation
> MapLibre 3D basemap with extruded buildings, Three.js procedural car / backpack traveler, a glowing route ribbon and a 2D→3D fly-in camera.
>
> ■ Follows your real pace
> Navigation advances with your real GPS position and walking speed (snap-to-road, smoothed) — it never jumps to the destination before you arrive. Offline voice guidance in Chinese/Japanese.
>
> ■ Travel essentials
> Live weather, real-time TWD↔JPY exchange rate and converter, subway/transit pass tips, and hotel picks across three budgets.
>
> ■ Bilingual (Chinese / English) interface.
>
> Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); vector tiles by OpenFreeMap.
